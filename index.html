About My Homeland

A country close to my heart.

Proudly from Nepal

I come from Nepal, a country nestled in the majestic Himalayas and famously known as the birthplace of Lord Buddha and home to Mount Everest. Nepal is a land of incredible natural beauty, peaceful landscapes, and breathtaking mountains.

Beyond nature, Nepal is deeply rich in vibrant traditions, colorful festivals like Dashain and Tihar, and a unique cultural heritage where diverse communities live together in harmony. Our culture is defined by warm hospitality, where guests are treated with utmost respect ("Atithi Devo Bhava").

Moving from Nepal to South Korea for my studies has been an inspiring journey—allowing me to bridge two wonderful cultures, expand my technical knowledge, and work toward a bright future.
